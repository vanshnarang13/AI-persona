# Experience: Head, Zero To One — E-Cell, IIT Roorkee

## Role and Duration

Head of Zero To One at the Entrepreneurship Cell (E-Cell), IIT Roorkee, since May 2025. Zero To One is the product and engineering vertical of the organization, responsible for building and maintaining technology products used across the IIT Roorkee ecosystem.

## What Zero To One Is

The name Zero To One comes from Peter Thiel's idea of creating something fundamentally new rather than simply replicating existing solutions. Our team builds products from scratch—starting with problem discovery and user research, moving through design and engineering, and ultimately deploying and maintaining production systems used by real students.

Unlike typical student projects, our products serve active users and solve operational challenges at scale. We build using production-grade technologies.

## Products Built Under My Leadership

### Campus Recruitment Platform

The largest product developed by the team is a campus recruitment platform currently used by more than 50 student clubs and organizations at IIT Roorkee. The platform manages the entire recruitment lifecycle, including opportunity postings, applications, assessments, shortlisting, and communication.

Before its launch, most clubs relied on Google Forms, spreadsheets, and WhatsApp groups, creating a fragmented and inefficient process. I led product strategy and engineering execution, working closely with stakeholders to prioritize features, define workflows, and ensure the platform could reliably support large-scale recruitment cycles across campus.

### Noosphere

I co-founded Noosphere as an initiative within Zero To One to bridge the gap between IIT Roorkee alumni founders and current students interested in entrepreneurship.

The platform creates a structured network for mentorship, collaboration, and knowledge sharing by connecting students with alumni who have founded startups and built successful companies. I defined the product vision and then worked on building the initial platform, and established relationships with alumni to create the founding network. This was later shut down due to absence of operational bandwidth.

### E-Summit 2026 Digital ID System

For E-Summit 2026, IIT Roorkee's flagship entrepreneurship festival inspired by Startup Mahakumbh, our team developed an automated digital ID card generation and verification system.

The platform streamlined attendee onboarding and identity management, significantly reducing manual effort for organizers while enabling smooth event operations at scale. I oversaw product planning, system design, and technical execution.

### Mess Menu & Rebate Management Platform

To improve day-to-day student services, our team built a platform that allows students to view daily mess menus, track meal schedules, and apply for hostel mess rebates through a centralized interface.

The application digitized processes that were previously scattered across notices and manual workflows, making routine student interactions more transparent and efficient. I provided product direction and technical oversight throughout development and deployment.


## What This Role Actually Involves

Managing a 15+ member cross-functional team requires much more than writing code. My responsibilities span product management, engineering leadership, stakeholder communication, and execution.

On the engineering side, I make architectural decisions, review critical implementations, define development standards, and help team members overcome technical bottlenecks. On the product side, I work directly with students, clubs, and organizers to understand problems, prioritize solutions, and translate requirements into actionable engineering plans.

One of the biggest challenges is maintaining execution velocity within a student organization where members balance academics, internships, and personal commitments. Delivering successful products in that environment requires leadership, ownership, and the ability to align people around a shared vision.
